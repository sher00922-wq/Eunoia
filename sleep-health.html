Why sleep health matters

Sleep challenges affect more than the night.

Sleep disruption and fatigue can influence daytime functioning, workplace safety, attendance, recovery and confidence returning to work. The Eunoia Praxis Sleep Health Program is being developed to make practical sleep and fatigue support easier to understand and access.

02Shift-work and changing schedules

04Recovery and rehabilitation

Who we support

Support shaped around different needs.

Whether you are seeking support for yourself or coordinating workplace, disability or rehabilitation services, we begin by understanding the concern, setting and intended outcome.

Individual support

Individuals and shift workers

For individuals experiencing sleep concerns, fatigue, changing schedules or difficulty developing sustainable sleep routines.

  • Difficulty maintaining consistent sleep routines
  • Shift-work and schedule-related sleep challenges
  • Fatigue affecting daily functioning
  • Insomnia-related concerns
  • Sleep needs during recovery or Return-to-Work
Referral & workplace support

Organizations and referral partners

For organizations and professionals seeking sleep and fatigue support for employees, clients or workplace teams.

  • Employers and human resources teams
  • Insurers and disability case managers
  • Legal professionals
  • Rehabilitation providers
  • Healthcare and occupational health partners
Program services

Practical support built around real routines.

01

Sleep education and coaching

Personalized education and practical strategies that support healthier and more sustainable sleep habits.

02

03

Fatigue management

Strategies that consider alertness, workload, daily demands, recovery opportunities and workplace safety.

CBT-I-informed sleep strategies

06

Disability and Return-to-Work support

Sleep-focused support within rehabilitation, disability management and sustainable Return-to-Work planning.

Frequently asked questions

What to know before launch.

When will the Sleep Health Program launch?

The program is currently in development. Eunoia Praxis is accepting inquiries from individuals and organizations interested in receiving updates or discussing potential needs.

Can individuals contact Eunoia Praxis directly?

Yes. Individuals may inquire directly about program availability and potential fit.

Can employers, insurers and rehabilitation professionals make referrals?

Yes. We welcome inquiries regarding individual referrals, workplace education and organizational programming.

Is this a medical sleep clinic?

No. The program does not replace medical assessment, diagnosis or treatment. If a concern may require medical evaluation, an appropriate referral may be recommended.

Will services be virtual or in person?

Delivery options, service locations and geographic availability will be confirmed as the program is finalized.

Are services covered by insurance?

Services may be eligible for reimbursement through an extended health benefits or insurance plan, depending on the terms of the individual policy and the qualifications of the professional delivering the service. Clients are responsible for contacting their insurance provider before beginning services to confirm eligibility, coverage limits and reimbursement requirements. Payment is made directly to Eunoia Praxis, and a receipt can be provided for submission to the insurance provider. Eunoia Praxis cannot guarantee coverage or reimbursement.
