Privacy & Confidentiality

Respecting privacy through confidential and responsible information practices.

Eunoia Praxis is committed to protecting personal information across all assessment, rehabilitation, and clinical service environments.

Confidentiality & responsible care.

Collection & Use

Personal information is collected only as necessary to provide assessments, reporting, rehabilitation services, and clinical support. Information is used solely within the scope of professional care delivery and case coordination.

Consent

Information is managed in accordance with applicable privacy legislation, professional standards, and informed consent practices across clinical, rehabilitation, and assessment services.

Disclosure

Information is only shared with authorized parties when consent, legal authority, or professional obligations permit or require disclosure.

Security

Appropriate administrative, technical, and physical safeguards are maintained to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, or loss.
